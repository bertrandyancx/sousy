        YOUR DATA

        Privacy and data

        Sous-easy keeps your cooking information understandable and under your control.

        What Sous-easy stores

        Your account email, username, profile details, follows, saved recipes, and recipes you upload are stored in Supabase so they can follow you across devices.

        What stays on your device

        Voice preferences, theme preferences, and some local recipe history are stored in your browser. Clearing browser data can remove those local items.

        AI recipe planning

        When you ask Sous-easy to build a plan, the recipe source is sent through Portkey to the configured planning model. Never upload private information that does not belong in a recipe.

        HEAR IT YOUR WAY

        Voice and accessibility

        Choose how Sous-easy speaks and how you respond while cooking.

        Voice instructions

        Read each cooking action aloud automatically.

        Exact voices depend on what your browser and device provide. If a requested voice is unavailable, Sous-easy chooses the closest match.

        About Sous-easy

        Good cooking, no thinking. It’s Sous-easy.

        ✿

        A calmer way to cook

        Sous-easy turns recipes into explicit, timed, voice-led guidance for people who want to cook without holding the whole plan in their head.

        Built for real kitchens

        It names the tools, temperatures, timing, safety moments, cleanup, and next action—then listens when something changes.
