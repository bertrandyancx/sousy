Sous-easy

Good cooking, no thinking.

A multi-agent kitchen expeditor for newer cooks

Bertrand Yancx · sous-easy.onrender.com

The problem

Recipes are reference documents, not real-time kitchen copilots.

Too much to track

Ingredients, pans, heat, order, timing, and safety all compete for attention.

Beginner friction

“Make the sauce” hides quantities, equipment, technique, and completion criteria.

Things change

Burning, missing ingredients, and slow prep require an immediate plan adjustment.

What Sous-easy does

Give it a recipe. Hear one exact next action. Do it. Report what happened. Sous-easy adapts.
  1. Import URL, PDF, or photo.
  2. Extract exact ingredients and equipment.
  3. Coordinate parallel cooking components.
  4. Speak and display one atomic action at a time.
  5. Move completed recipes to Past recipes.

Why it is different

Recipe and meal-planning apps are an overdone category. The twist is the execution model.

Compared with Paprika, SideChef, Samsung Food, and ChatGPT

Those products are strong at saving, discovering, planning, or conversing about recipes. Sous-easy is designed to coordinate the physical execution moment.

Example

“Take 1 tablespoon soy sauce and measure it into the bowl.”

Then: “Measure 2 tablespoons water into the bowl.”

Then: “Add 1 teaspoon honey and stir until blended.”

It behaves like an expeditor coordinating stations, not a chatbot rewriting a recipe.

Safety by design

RiskDefense
Prompt injection in recipe sourceSource is untrusted data; role changes and secret requests are ignored.
Malformed planJSON shape validation before browser scheduling.
Smoke / unsafe heatDeterministic heat-off action takes priority over AI recovery.
Overspending8-call budget, input cap, rate limit, usage logs.
Physical harmModel advises; human controls all real equipment.

Tokenomics + impact

Measured initial plan: 6 calls, 8,604 input tokens, 10,248 output tokens.

Recovery test: 181 input tokens, 247 output tokens.

Cost formula: tokens × official course price. The app exposes /api/metrics for evidence.

Next pilot: compare a newer cook using a normal recipe against Sous-easy. Measure questions, missed steps, completion, time, safety corrections, and confidence.
